About SGSITS Indore

Govt. Aided Autonomous

Established in 1952, Shri Govindram Seksaria Institute of Technology and Science (SGSITS) is Central India’s pioneer engineering institution. Situated across an iconic 35-acre green expanse in the heart of Indore near Nehru Park and the railway terminus, the autonomous grant-in-aid college functions under a board of governors nominated by the Government of Madhya Pradesh and industry leaders.

Holding academic autonomy with degrees awarded by Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya (RGPV) and Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), SGSITS enjoys top-tier placement records with tech companies (Amazon, Microsoft, Goldman Sachs, TCS, Infosys) and leading core engineering firms (L&T, Tata Motors, Asian Paints). With over 7 decades of research output, it remains Madhya Pradesh's most competitive college in JEE Main state-level MP DTE counseling.
