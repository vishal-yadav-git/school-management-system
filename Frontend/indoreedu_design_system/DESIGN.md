## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a confident, trustworthy, and modern educational discovery platform tailored for students, parents, and educators navigating academic institutions in Indore. 

The aesthetic marries institutional authority with high-clarity consumer utility:
- **Tone & Mood:** Trustworthy, modern, clean, organized, and energetic. The UI communicates clarity and academic ambition without feeling sterile or academic-heavy.
- **Visual Style:** Modern Flat Surface System. Driven by crisp flat color planes, generous whitespace, structured card grids, and high-legibility typography. Strictly avoid complex multi-stop gradients, skeuomorphic depth, 3D blobs, glassmorphism, and illustrative emojis.
- **Iconography:** Cohesive 1.5px to 2px stroke thin-line icons (neutral or tinted indigo), always geometrically crisp and strictly functional.

## Colors

The palette balances deep academic purples against soft lavender tinted planes and decisive high-energy accent triggers:

- **Primary Indigo-Purple (`#5B4BC4`):** The functional core. Used for brand markers, active navigation states, selected pill filters, secondary action buttons, and focused borders.
- **Deep Purple (`#2A1F6B`):** Anchoring contrast color. Applied to persistent sidebars, high-impact hero promo blocks, premium banner surfaces, and major section headings requiring supreme authority.
- **Accent Orange (`#F26B3A`):** High-priority signal. Strictly reserved for conversion drivers (e.g., "Apply Now", "Book Free Demo"), flash announcements, and high-visibility spotlight tags. Must never be diluted through decorative ambient usage.
- **Surfaces & Canvas:** 
  - Main Canvas: Soft Lavender Tint (`#EFECFB`) to provide warmth and eye-comfort during long browsing sessions.
  - Interactive Surfaces & Content Containers: Pure Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Subtle Borders & Separators: Hairline Border Tint (`#E2DEFA`).
- **Typography & Neutrals:**
  - Heading & Primary Body: Ink Dark Navy (`#1B1A33`).
  - Supporting & Secondary Metadata: Slate Muted Indigo (`#5E5C7A`).
- **Semantic Feedback:**
  - Success Green (`#1F9D6B`): Institutional verification badges, accreditation confirmations, active admissions status indicators.

## Typography

The design system exclusively adopts **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all textual hierarchies. Generic neo-grotesques such as Inter or Roboto are explicitly prohibited to maintain a bespoke contemporary consumer character.

- **Headings & Titles:** Set with deliberate negative letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold) to deliver impact, energy, and rapid scanability.
- **Body & Editorial Content:** Set at Regular (`400`) weight with natural line-heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) on ink-tinted charcoal (`#1B1A33`) or secondary slate (`#5E5C7A`) to preserve effortless legibility across dense educational directories.
- **Pills, Badges & Labels:** Set in Medium to Bold (`600` - `700`) with neutral or slight positive letter-spacing (`0.01em` - `0.03em`) for immediate recognition inside compact UI tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm using a fluid-responsive column grid system.

- **Breakpoints & Grids:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem (16px)`, `gutter: 1rem (16px)`. Single-column feeds with edge-to-edge touch carousels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin-md: 2rem (32px)`, `gutter-sm: 1rem (16px)`. Dual-column grids for institute listings and catalog results.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered, `margin-lg: 4rem (64px)`, `gutter-lg: 2rem (32px)`. Multi-column directory listings with persistent filter sidebars and multi-card showcase carousels.
- **Whitespace Discipline:** Generous section spacing (`space-xl` / 40px) between horizontal rail carousels to prevent information fatigue when displaying dense institutional metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on crisp surface layering combined with soft, lavender-tinted ambient shadows. Avoid murky neutral gray drop shadows and harsh outlines.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the main page background (`#EFECFB`). No shadow, no border.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` card surfaces sitting on `#EFECFB`. Framed with a 1px border of `#E2DEFA` and supported by an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(42, 31, 107, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):** Applied during card hover states and active discovery listings: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(42, 31, 107, 0.08)`, border transforms to `#5B4BC4` at 40% opacity with a -2px vertical lift.
- **Level 3 (Floating Headers, Popovers & Search Overlays):** Used for sticky category navbars, autocomplete dropdowns, and floating search modals: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -6px rgba(42, 31, 107, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances approachable modern curvature with structured spatial economy:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Standard roundedness uses 16px (`rounded-lg`) to 24px (`rounded-xl`) corner radii, ensuring cards feel friendly and premium while holding structured content blocks.
- **Buttons, Quick Filters & Search Bars:** Always fully rounded (pill-shaped / `border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Locality, Verified & Status Badges:** Compact pill-shaped profiles (`border-radius: 9999px`) with symmetric horizontal padding.
- **Internal Media & Thumbnails:** Framed with an inner radius of 12px to 16px to echo card boundary proportions.

## Components

### 1. Pill Search Bar & Quick Filters
- **Pill Search Input:** A prominent 56px high input with a `9999px` border radius, white background (`#FFFFFF`), subtle border (`#E2DEFA`), thin-line search icon in `#5B4BC4`, and clear action trigger. Integrated dropdown hints for localities (Vijay Nagar, Bhawarkuan, Palasia, Rau).
- **Quick Filter Pills:** 36px height, pill-shaped. Unselected: `#FFFFFF` surface, `#1B1A33` text, `#E2DEFA` border. Selected: `#5B4BC4` background, `#FFFFFF` text, zero border.

### 2. Category Nav Bar
- Horizontally scrollable or fixed ribbon featuring high-level categories (e.g., Engineering, Medical, CBSE Schools, Competitive Exams, Coaching).
- Icons are 20px clean line-art enclosed in soft lavender circles (`#EFECFB`). Active categories display an active `#5B4BC4` bottom anchor pill indicator.

### 3. Deep Purple Hero & Promo Cards
- Background: `#2A1F6B` with white and lavender typography.
- Layout: 24px border radius with rich contrast. Accompanied by integrated action buttons using `#F26B3A`.
- Carousel Indicators: Pill-shaped active dot (`16px x 6px` in `#F26B3A`) and muted circular dots (`6px x 6px` in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).

### 4. Institute Listing Cards
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), 16px padding, 20px border radius, 1px border in `#E2DEFA`.
- Header: Institute thumbnail (rounded 12px) paired with Verified Badge (Solid green `#1F9D6B` with white checkmark) and Star Rating pill (Gold star with `#1B1A33` rating score).
- Locality Tags: Compact pills (`#EFECFB` background, `#5B4BC4` text) displaying Indore micro-markets: *Vijay Nagar*, *Palasia*, *Bhawarkuan*, *Rau*, *Geeta Bhawan*.
- Footer: Clear fee/course snippet and dual CTA buttons ("View Brochure" in neutral outline, "Apply Now" in Accent Orange `#F26B3A`).

### 5. Horizontal Card Carousels
- Overflowing horizontal rails with hidden native scrollbars, accompanied by floating navigation arrows (40px white circular discs, `#E2DEFA` border, thin-line purple chevron) centered along card edges.

### 6. KPI Metric Cards
- Compact data blocks highlighting platform statistics (e.g., "500+ Verified Institutes", "50,000+ Students Guided").
- Pure white background, 16px radius, bold numeric counters set in `#2A1F6B` (`display-lg`), and subtle muted captions in `#5E5C7A`.

### 7. Buttons & Form Elements
- **Primary CTAs:** Solid Accent Orange (`#F26B3A`), white text, bold font, pill-shaped (`9999px` radius).
- **Secondary Buttons:** Deep Indigo (`#5B4BC4`) background, white text, pill-shaped.
- **Tertiary / Outline Buttons:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px `#E2DEFA` border, `#5B4BC4` text.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px size with 6px radius (checkbox) or circle (radio); checked state in solid `#5B4BC4` with crisp white check/dot.
